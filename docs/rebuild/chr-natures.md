## Rule (static)

- `base` = byte 0. Battle init 0x02077C0C writes it to char+0x13 (strb at 0x02077C7C).
- Koma size s (4..8): B = byte `0x10+(s-4)*4+2` (write 0x02077C84 to char+0x14); C = byte `0x10+(s-4)*4+3` (write 0x02077C8C to char+0x15).
- The panel override of A (0x02076FA4, from koma[0xB]>>4 when not 3) changes char+0x13 only. B and C never see the panel.
- Slot selection for a hit is from its KB mask (arm9 table T18 at 0x0209FDC4: mask bit 10 (0x400) -> selector bit 6 -> B, bit 11 (0x800) -> selector bit 7 -> C; neither -> A). When both 0x400 and 0x800 are set, B wins (no KB record in docs/rebuild/knockback-table.json has both). The exporter does that, not this table.
- The `captured` list names the captures of set-hit slots (Eve chr 37, E3b; Train chr 38, TRX), so the exporter can grade those slots measured.

The arm9 cites are a static trace (a Sonnet pass over the disassembly). The E3b selectors and the 0x15 readings agree (docs/research/feel-captures/2026-10-12-eve-mash/README.md Goal B).
